1. What the App collects

When the channel owner signs in with TikTok, the App receives an access token, a refresh token and the owner's TikTok open ID. No other personal information is requested or collected.

2. How it is used

These tokens are used only to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts (scope: video.upload). The App does not read, collect or analyse any other TikTok data, and does not access any other user's account.

3. Where it is stored

The tokens are stored only in a local file on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server operated by the App, and there is no such server.

4. Sharing

No data is sold, shared or transferred to any third party.

5. Deletion

The owner can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions) and delete the local token file.

6. Contact

Questions can be sent through the HappyThought YouTube channel: @happythought_kr.
