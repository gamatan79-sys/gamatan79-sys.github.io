HappyThought (행복생각) is a creator channel that publishes short animated videos about one sentence from classic philosophy each day — for example Seneca, Schopenhauer and Baltasar Gracián — applied to everyday life.

About the HappyThought Uploader app

The HappyThought Uploader is a private tool that runs only on the channel owner's own computer. After the owner signs in with TikTok and grants permission, it sends finished videos to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts. The owner reviews each draft in the TikTok app and publishes it manually.

The app is used only by the channel owner. It is not offered to the public and does not access any other user's account.
